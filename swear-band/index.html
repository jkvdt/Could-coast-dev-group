The honest answer: the rubber band trick works because it's immediate, physical, and slightly annoying. A real habit tool just makes sure that jolt is consistent — same snap, same reminder, every single time — instead of whatever's rattling around in your desk drawer that day.

Before you order

Questions, answered directly.

Does it actually hurt?

No — it's built to be noticeable, not painful. Think firm snap, not injury. The goal is a jolt sharp enough that your brain files it under "don't do that again," nothing more.

What's it made of?

A thicker silicone-blend band, cut wider and rounder at the edges than a standard office rubber band so it snaps flat against skin instead of pinching a fold of it.

What size is it?

One universal adult size, fitting roughly a 5.5"–8" wrist. Let us know in your order notes if you need a petite or XL fit — we're sizing a second run based on early demand.

Can I choose my own saying?

Yes. Every pack lets you pick from the full saying library, and multi-packs can mix and match — no two bands have to say the same thing.

What if one snaps or wears out?

We'll replace it free within 90 days of normal use. That's the deal — a habit tool that quits on you before the habit does isn't worth wearing.

Do you do bulk or team orders?

Absolutely — classrooms, offices, and sports teams running a group "swear jar" challenge get a discount past 10 bands. Join the list below and mention it in your first email.

When do bands actually ship?

We're pre-launch and finalizing our first production run now. Reserve your pack and we'll email the moment bands are ready to ship — pricing shown today is honored for everyone on the list.
